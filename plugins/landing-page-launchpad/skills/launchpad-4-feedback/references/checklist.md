# Checklist

Twelve checks that the builder does not satisfy by itself. Check the built page, not your memory of it. Report each as in Ordnung, behoben or offen.

1. First screen answers: what is this, for whom, what do I do next.
2. Headline, announcement text and link preview make the same promise.
3. One goal; every action button has the same wording and leads to the same form.
4. Every factual sentence is covered by „Was auf die Seite darf"; assumptions appear only as scene, stance or intention.
5. Date, time, place, price and limits match the strategy digit by digit.
6. No invented numbers, quotes, logos, deadlines or scarcity; no generated people shown as real.
7. Du or Sie is consistent everywhere, including form messages.
8. No placeholder text; no phrase from the machine-German list in the playbook.
9. Headings read top to bottom tell the argument.
10. At phone width nothing is cut off and the action is reachable on the first screen.
11. The page loads nothing from third-party servers; fonts come from the project or the fallback is reported.
12. The Mockup note is visible and submitting stores and sends nothing.

Always list, do not fix silently: missing proof or images („Fehlt noch"), Impressum and Datenschutz as placeholders, real sign-up not connected, Feedback-Gespräche not yet held.
