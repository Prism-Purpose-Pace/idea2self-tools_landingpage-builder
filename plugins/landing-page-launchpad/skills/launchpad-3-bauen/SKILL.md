# Etappe 3 · Entwerfen und bauen (about 90 min including reserve)

Turn the confirmed strategy into a Seitenkonzept, then into a complete clickable Entwurf, improve it once, and prepare the Feedback-Gespräche. This is the last part of the workshop session.

Read [references/core-rules.md](references/core-rules.md) first. Work from `docs/Produkt-Strategie_v1.md`.

| Block | Content | Guide time | Reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marke, Richtung, Bilder | 10 min | [references/art-direction.md](references/art-direction.md) |
| 2 | Seitenkonzept und Abschluss | 15 min | [references/page-playbook.md](references/page-playbook.md), [assets/template-landing-page-konzept.md](assets/template-landing-page-konzept.md) |
| 3 | Bau | 15 min | [references/build-rules.md](references/build-rules.md) |
| 4 | Überarbeitung 1 | 25 min | [references/checklist.md](references/checklist.md) |
| 5 | Feedback-Gespräche vorbereiten | 10 min | [references/interview-guide.md](references/interview-guide.md) |

Blocks 1 and 2 run in Chat-Modus. Build-Modus is needed three times: for the build, for the Überarbeitung, for the guide.

## Block 1 von 5 · Marke, Richtung, Bilder

Read the art direction reference before asking; it decides what a usable answer looks like.

1. Hast du schon eine Marke (Logo, Farben, Schriften, Leitsatz), oder soll ich einen Entwurf vorschlagen? Wenn ja: schick, was du hast.
2. Gibt es eine Seite, die dir gefällt, und eine, die dir nicht gefällt? Was genau daran?
3. Welche eigenen Fotos kannst du jetzt hochladen (du, dein Ort, deine Arbeit)? Was soll auf Bildern auf keinen Fall zu sehen sein?
4. Gibt es No-Gos, die für dich als Anbieter wichtig sind?

With a brand: adopt its values exactly, ask only for what is missing, and still fix composition and signature element as the reference describes.

Without a brand: propose two or three directions that differ in character, built as the reference describes, each opened with one sentence of plain imagery so a non-designer can choose. All are `[Vorschlag KI]`. The user picks or mixes.

Where the user's taste conflicts with what suits the audience, say so once; the provider has to stand behind the page.

Images: real photos of the provider and the real setting come first. Generated or stock images may show a mood or a situation, never invented customers or results. A generated image that could pass for a real photo of real people, places or events gets a visible note; record it in the image plan.

## Block 2 von 5 · Seitenkonzept und Abschluss

1. Read the playbook and fill the concept template: argument line first, then the sections that the argument needs.
2. Write the real texts in the chosen form of address, from the user's wording and the `[Zitat Zielgruppe]` lines. No placeholder text. Every factual sentence must be covered by „Was auf die Seite darf".
3. Offer three headline candidates from different angles (see playbook) and mark your recommendation with the reason.
4. Write the announcement text for the Hauptkanal and the link preview text so they match the headline.
5. Run the three text tests from the playbook on your own draft before showing it.
6. Show in chat, in full: the first screen (headline, subline, button, line under the button), the problem section and the text about the provider. Show the rest as an outline: section headings in order, design direction in two lines, image plan. Then: „Lies die drei Texte einmal laut. Klingt das nach dir? Sollen wir genau das bauen? Alle weiteren Texte siehst du gleich auf der Seite und kannst sie dort direkt ändern."
7. Apply corrections. Then ask the user to switch to Build-Modus and write `bauen`.

The confirmed Seitenkonzept is the plan; no separate planning round.

## Block 3 von 5 · Bau

One Build turn does all of this: save the Seitenkonzept and the status file, update `AGENTS.md` with the Etappe 3 lines from [assets/template-agents-brief.md](assets/template-agents-brief.md), and build the complete page by the build rules.

Before the turn starts, give the user something to do: „Der Bau dauert ein paar Minuten. Schreib in der Zeit drei bis fünf Namen aus deiner Zielgruppe auf, die du in den nächsten Tagen 15 Minuten lang fragen kannst."

Afterwards report in three lines: what was built, what was not changed (texts), what is open (image placeholders). Say that the sign-up is a Mockup that stores and sends nothing, and how to look: Vorschau at phone width first, then desktop.

## Block 4 von 5 · Überarbeitung 1

This round removes what the author and the checklist can find, so the Feedback-Gespräche are spent on what only other people see. In a workshop room it is also the moment for a peer test: two neighbours swap laptops for a few minutes and answer the three questions for each other.

1. Ask, in Chat-Modus:
   - „Was würde jemand, der dich nicht kennt, nach fünf Sekunden über dein Angebot sagen?"
   - „An welcher Stelle würdest du selbst aufhören zu lesen?"
   - „Welcher Satz klingt nicht nach dir?"
2. Run the checklist against the built page.
3. Merge both into one short numbered list, grouped by kind: Text, Layout, Gestaltung, Bild, Strategie. Point out which text changes the user can make directly in the Vorschau at no cost.
4. A Strategie point is not fixed on the page: clarify it in the strategy first (`_v2`), then change the page.
5. Agree the list, then make all changes of one kind in one request, say what stays untouched, and confirm what changed. Usually one Build turn is enough. A vague wish („mach es schöner") gets one clarifying question first.
6. Suggest bookmarking the current version in the version history before a larger redesign.

From now on the page owns the wording: the user may edit texts freely on it. The Seitenkonzept keeps owning structure and what may be claimed; a new factual claim on the page needs a line in „Was auf die Seite darf".

In `kurzfassung`, fix checklist failures only. Stop when the list is done; a first Entwurf is ready when the argument is complete and nothing is broken.

## Block 5 von 5 · Feedback-Gespräche vorbereiten und Abschluss

1. Take the names the user noted during the build and ask for dates. Keep names in chat only, never in project files.
2. Build the guide from the interview reference: the fixed script in the page's form of address, plus two or three questions specific to this page, taken from the accepted risks and tests in the strategy. One page.
3. Explain in one sentence how the Entwurf reaches the other person without publishing: on the user's own screen, or by sharing the screen in a call.
4. One Build turn: `docs/Interviewleitfaden_v1.md` and the status file. If credits are gone, print the guide in chat for copying.
5. Close: what exists now, that the sign-up is still a Mockup and the page must not be announced, and the two lines for later: `/launchpad-4-feedback` after the Feedback-Gespräche, `/launchpad-5-veroeffentlichen` before going live.

Do not publish and do not call the page ready to launch.

## Guardrails

- No code before the Seitenkonzept is confirmed.
- Directions must differ in character, not only in colour.
- While building, do not shorten, embellish or add claims. If a text does not fit, change the layout or ask.
- Real people only as real people.
- The session ends with names and dates. Without them the Feedback-Gespräche usually do not happen.

## Examples

```text
Änderungswunsch schwach: „Mach die Seite moderner und emotionaler."
Änderungswunsch stark:   „Text: Subline im ersten Bildschirm auf zwei Zeilen kürzen; zweite FAQ-Antwort
                          streichen. Layout: Abstand über ‚So läuft es ab' verdoppeln. Sonst nichts ändern."

Einordnen: „Eigentlich will ich eher Teams ansprechen als Einzelne."
→ Strategie, nicht Text. Erst Zielgruppe in Strategie_v2 klären, dann Überschrift ändern.
```
